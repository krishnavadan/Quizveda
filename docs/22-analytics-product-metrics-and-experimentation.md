# Product Analytics and Experimentation

## North-star candidate
Weekly meaningful learning/play sessions per active user, balanced with completion, repeat use and content quality. Avoid optimizing only for time spent or streak pressure.

## Funnel
catalog.viewed → mode.selected → session.created → question.issued → answer.accepted → session.completed → explanation.viewed → topic.practice.started.

## Metrics
Activation, completion, 7/28-day return, explanation engagement, topic revisit, question flag/skip rate, item difficulty distribution, answer ambiguity, locale availability, latency and reliability. Do not log answer keys or raw tokens.

## Experiments
Pre-register hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, audience and duration. Avoid shame-based streaks, gambling-like reward loops and high-stakes outcome experiments. Privacy-review segmentation; use only non-sensitive product attributes; provide kill switch.
